# DigiInvi - Application Feature Overview

## 1. Introduction
DigiInvi is a comprehensive, modern billing and financial management solution designed to streamline business operations. Built with cross-platform technology (Android, iOS, Web), it offers a seamless experience for managing invoices, clients, expenses, and financial health—all in one place.

## 2. Core Modules & Features

### A. Secure Authentication & Access
*   **Secure Login**: Cloud-based authentication ensuring data is safe and accessible only to authorized users.
*   **Biometric Security**: Advanced option to unlock the app using **Fingerprint** or **Face ID** for quick and secure access.
*   **PIN Protection**: 4-digit PIN lock support as an alternative to biometrics, ensuring application-level security even if the device is unlocked.

### B. Smart Billing & Invoicing
At the heart of DigiBills is a powerful invoicing engine:
*   **Instant Invoice Generation**: Create professional bills in seconds.
*   **Auto-Incrementing Invoice Numbers**: Automatically handles unique invoice numbering (e.g., `INV-1001`).
*   **Client Integration**: Select existing clients or add new ones instantly during bill creation.
*   **Multi-Item Support**: Add multiple products or services to a single bill with individual pricing.
*   **Advance Payment Tracking**: Record advance amounts received; the app automatically calculates the net payable balance.
*   **Tax/GST Handling**: Input tax percentages, and the app auto-calculates the tax amount and total tax liability.
*   **Payment Status Tracking**: Mark invoices as **Paid**, **Pending**, or **Partial**.
*   **PDF Generation**: Generate professional, branded PDF invoices ready for printing.
*   **Direct Sharing**: Share invoices directly via **WhatsApp** or other messaging apps from within the bill creation screen.

### C. Client Management
*   **Client Database**: Maintain a detailed directory of all clients.
*   **Client Details**: Store essential info including Name, Address, and Contact Number.
*   **Smart IDs**: System-generated Client IDs for easy referencing.
*   **One-Tap Communication**: Call or WhatsApp clients directly from their profile.

### D. Expense Tracking
*   **Digital Expense Log**: Move away from paper notebooks. Record all business expenses digitally.
*   **Categorization**: Organize expenses to understand where money is going.
*   **Search & History**: Easily view and search past expenses.

### E. Comprehensive Accounting Reports
Gain deep insights into business performance with a dedicated Reports module:
*   **Profit & Loss (PnL)**: Real-time calculation of Income vs. Expenses to show Net Profit.
*   **Tax Ledger**: Detailed report of all tax collected, aiding in GST/Tax filing.
*   **Receivables Report**: A dedicated view for **Outstanding Payments**—know exactly who owes you money and how much.
*   **Year-End Summary**: High-level summary of the entire financial year.
*   **Data Export**: Export reports to **CSV** or **Excel** formats for audit purposes or external accounting software.

### F. Calendar & Scheduling
*   **Event Scheduler**: Integrated calendar to keep track of important billing dates, follow-ups, or business events.
*   **Visual Planner**: Month-view calendar to visualize upcoming tasks.

### G. Settings & Personalization
*   **Business Profile**: Customize the app with your **Business Name**, **Address**, **Contact Email**, and **Phone**.
*   **Brand Customization**: Upload and resize your **Business Logo** to appear on invoices.
*   **Bank Details**: Store complex bank info (Account No, IFSC, Bank Name) once and have it ready for invoices.
*   **UPI Integration**: Save **UPI ID** and **Payee Name** to facilitate digital payments.

## 3. Technical Highlights
*   **Cross-Platform**: Works flawlessly on Android, iOS, and Web.
*   **Cloud Sync**: All data is stored securely in the cloud (Firebase), allowing real-time synchronization across devices.
*   **Modern UI/UX**: clean, intuitive, and professional interface designed for ease of use.
