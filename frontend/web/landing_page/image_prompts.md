# Image Prompts for DigiInvi Redesign

Use these prompts with a high-quality AI image generator (like Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, or similar) to create the assets for your new 3D website.

## General Style
**Style Keywords:** 3D render, claymorphism or glassmorphism, vibrant gradient lighting (purple, blue, neon cyan), isometric view, highly detailed, smooth textures, professional, tech-focused, minimalist background.
